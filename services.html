How we work

Four stages, every engagement

A fixed shape makes estimates honest and progress visible. Duration varies; the sequence does not.

Discovery

Workshops with the people who do the work. Current state, constraints, data quality, integration points and the outcome you are actually buying.

Design

Target architecture or process design, written down with the trade-offs made explicit, plus a plan, a cost view and acceptance criteria.

Build

Two-week cycles, working increments, testing as we go. You see the system in your own data long before cutover.

Run

Operations, support and optimisation against service levels, with monthly reporting and a named service manager.

Engagement models

Most clients combine two: a fixed-scope build, then a managed service once it is live.

Model Best for How it is priced
Advisory sprint Assessments, architecture reviews, roadmaps and cost cases Fixed fee, two to six weeks
Fixed-scope project Implementations and migrations with a clear definition of done Fixed price against an agreed scope, milestone based
Managed service Ongoing operations, application support and back-office processing Monthly retainer by service level, volume or pod size
Dedicated pod Long-running work where you want capacity and continuity Monthly per seat or per role, minimum term
