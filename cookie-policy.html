What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in comparable ways. This policy covers both.

Your choice

When you first visit this site you are asked whether to allow analytics cookies. Essential cookies are always active because the site cannot work without them. You can change your choice at any time using the cookie settings link in the footer, or by clearing site data in your browser.

Cookies used on this site

Name Type Purpose Retention
corveon_cookie_consent Essential Records your cookie preference so you are not asked on every page. 12 months
Session cookie Essential Maintains state while you browse and helps protect form submissions. Until you close the browser
Analytics cookies Analytics (consent) Aggregate measurement of visits, pages viewed and referring sources, used to improve the site. Set only if you accept analytics. Up to 24 months

No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are used on this site.

Managing cookies in your browser

All major browsers let you see stored cookies, delete them and block them for specific sites. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of this site from working. Browser help pages explain the exact steps for Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox.

Third party content

If we embed content from another service, for example a video or a map, that provider may set its own cookies. Where such content requires consent, it is loaded only after you accept.

Questions

For anything about this policy, write to hello@corveon.in. For how we handle personal information more generally, see the privacy policy.
