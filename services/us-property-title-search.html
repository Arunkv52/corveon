Title research support that holds up under review

Back-office title search, examination support and abstracting for title agents, attorneys and lenders, delivered to your format and your turnaround.

Request a pilot

What this service solves

Title volume is seasonal and unforgiving. When orders spike, searchers work late and turnaround slips. When volume drops, fixed cost sits idle. Recruiting and training experienced searchers takes months either way.

Corveon runs title research as a managed pod. We learn your search standards, county sources and report format, document them, and staff against your volume. You keep examination and underwriting decisions; we do the research, the retrieval and the typing that sits underneath them.

Work is delivered into your system or your templates, so output reaches your examiners in the form they already read.

Who we support

Title agencies
Search and typing capacity through demand peaks.

Real estate attorneys
Chain of title and lien research for closings.

Lenders and servicers
Ownership, encumbrance and tax status checks.

Title technology firms
Research capacity behind a digital order flow.

What we do

🏠

Current owner search

Vesting deed, open mortgages and assignments, liens and judgements, and tax status, assembled to your search period.

🔎

Two-owner and limited search

Search to the period your product requires, with the transfers, encumbrances and releases in that window.

🔗

Full search and chain of title

Extended search building the chain across the required period, with gaps, breaks and clouds flagged for examination.

🧾

Tax and assessment research

Assessed values, current and delinquent taxes, special assessments and exemptions from county and municipal sources.

⚖️

Judgement and lien search

Name searches for judgements, federal and state tax liens, mechanics liens, HOA liens and relevant civil filings.

📂

Document retrieval

Recorded deeds, mortgages, assignments, releases, plats and court documents pulled and indexed against the file.

⌨️

Typing and abstracting

Search results typed into your abstract, report or commitment template, with legal descriptions transcribed and proofread.

📑

Commitment and policy support

Schedule A and Schedule B preparation support, requirement and exception drafting to your standards, ready for examiner review.

How we set up and run

01

Standards capture

Your search requirements, county sources, report formats and exception rules, documented as work instructions.

02

Training and calibration

Staff qualified against sample files with known answers before any live order is touched.

03

Paid pilot

A defined batch on live volume, reviewed file by file, with accuracy and turnaround measured against target.

Steady state

Agreed daily capacity, standard and rush turnaround, two-level checking and weekly reporting.

Review and scale
