Talk to us about CRM and ERP
Overview

What this service solves

Most failed ERP and CRM projects were not beaten by the software. They were beaten by a process that existed only in the implementation plan, data that was never cleaned, and a go-live where users quietly went back to spreadsheets.

We work the other way round. Discovery sits with the people doing the job: sales, accounts, stores, production, service. We map the process as it runs today, agree which parts should change and which are there for good reason, and then configure the platform to fit.

Zoho and ERPNext are both strong at this. Zoho suits organisations that want a connected suite quickly, with licensing that scales sensibly. ERPNext suits manufacturing and distribution businesses that need depth in inventory, production and costing, and value an open-source core they can extend.

Use cases

Where this work pays for itself

Spreadsheets at the limit

Orders, stock and invoicing tracked across several workbooks. One system, one set of numbers, month-end that closes in days rather than weeks.

Multi-entity and multi-location

Separate books per entity with consolidated reporting, inter-company transactions and consistent chart of accounts.

Quote to cash taking too long

Approvals, credit checks and invoicing wired into one flow so the delay between a won deal and an invoice is measured in hours.

Inventory nobody trusts

Cycle counts, bin locations, batch and serial tracking and reorder rules, so stock on the screen matches stock on the shelf.

Field service and after-sales

Job scheduling, technician apps, spare parts consumption and service contracts joined up with the finance system.

Approach

How we run implementations

01

Discover

Function-by-function workshops, as-is process maps, data assessment and a target design with the gaps listed and decided.

02

Configure

Build in short cycles with a demo at the end of each, using your own data and your own vocabulary so users recognise what they see.

03

Migrate

Cleanse, map, load in stages and reconcile. Nothing goes live on data the finance team has not signed off.

04

Train

Role-based sessions, written guides, super-users identified and briefed, plus support on the floor through the first cycle.

05

Support

Service desk, monthly enhancement pool and a quarterly review of what the business now needs that it did not need at go-live.
