Talk to us about data and AI

What this service solves

Reporting usually breaks in one of three places. The data never arrives reliably. The definitions differ by team, so two dashboards disagree about revenue. Or the visualisation was built for a board slide rather than for the person who has to act on it daily.

We fix the order of work. Define the measures with the people who own them. Engineer pipelines that are monitored and testable. Model the data so a metric has one definition. Only then design dashboards, and design them around a decision rather than around available fields.

AI follows the same discipline. We look for work that is repetitive, high volume and expensive, where a model plus a review step removes real effort. Forecasting, classification, document extraction and retrieval assistants earn their place. Anything that only demonstrates well does not.

Custom dashboards

Executive, operational and financial views designed around the decision they support, with drill-through to the transaction and a defined refresh cadence.

🧩

Embedded analytics

Reporting delivered inside your own product or portal, with row-level security so each customer sees only their data.

Forecasting and machine learning

Demand and revenue forecasting, churn and propensity scoring, anomaly detection, pricing support and segmentation, evaluated against a baseline before it goes anywhere near production.

Document AI and assistants

MLOps and monitoring

How we run data engagements

01

Frame

Agree the decisions the work must support, the measures behind them and how success will be judged.

02

Engineer

Build ingestion and transformation with tests and monitoring. Fix data quality at the source wherever it is possible.

03

Model

Define the shared semantic layer so every metric has one definition and one owner.

04

Visualise

Design and build the dashboards or the AI workflow, test them with real users, adjust before rollout.

05

Improve

Track usage, retire what nobody opens, tune models, extend coverage where the value showed up.
