Revenue cycle work, done on schedule

Billing and AR support for practices, hospitals and billing companies, run by trained staff inside your own practice management system, under documented controls.

Request a pilot

What this service solves

Revenue leaks in predictable places. Eligibility not checked before the visit. Charges entered late. Claims going out with avoidable errors. Denials worked only when someone finds time. AR ageing past the window where recovery is realistic.

None of that is complicated work. It is work that has to happen every day, in order, with someone accountable for the queue. That is what we provide: a trained pod working in your system, to your payer rules, with daily queue discipline and weekly reporting.

You keep clinical and coding authority. We keep the cycle moving and tell you honestly where it is stuck.

Physician practices

Health technology firms
Operations behind a billing product.

What we do

🪪

Eligibility and benefits verification

Coverage, plan status, copay, deductible and authorisation requirements checked ahead of the visit, with findings recorded in the patient record.

⌨️

Charge entry and claim scrubbing

Charges entered from documentation with modifier and demographic checks, scrubbed against payer rules before submission.

🩺

Coding support

CPT, ICD-10 and HCPCS support and documentation review by certified coders, with queries routed to the provider rather than guessed.

📤

Claim submission and clearing house management

Electronic and paper submission, rejection handling at the clearing house, and resubmission inside payer timelines.

💳

Payment posting and reconciliation

ERA and manual posting, allowance and adjustment accuracy, balancing to deposits and flagging underpayments.

🚫

Denial management and appeals

Denials categorised by reason, corrected and appealed with supporting documentation, and root causes fed back to the front end.

📞

AR follow-up

Ageing buckets worked on a defined cadence, payer calls and portal follow-up, documented notes and escalation of systemic issues.

🧾

Patient billing and credentialing support

Statements, patient balance queries, and support with payer enrolment, revalidation and credentialing paperwork.

Typical arrangements

AR that has aged past 90 days

A focused clean-up project on the ageing backlog, worked oldest-recoverable first, alongside business-as-usual billing.

Denials repeating by payer

Denials analysed by reason and payer, front-end rules changed, and the recurrence tracked to prove the fix.

A practice group adding locations

Billing capacity added without recruiting, with the same procedures applied across all sites.

Billing company under peak load

A dedicated pod on named clients, reported separately so your own client reviews stay clean.

How we set up and run

01

Assessment

Review of payer mix, current metrics, system configuration and the points in the cycle where value is leaking.

02

Procedure capture

Your workflow, payer rules and escalation paths documented as work instructions, with access provisioned to least privilege.

03

Pilot

A defined scope or location run for a fixed period, with clean claim rate, turnaround and accuracy measured.

04

Steady state

Daily queue discipline across eligibility, entry, submission, posting, denials and AR, with weekly reporting.

05

Review

Monthly review of collections, AR ageing, denial reasons and the front-end changes that would prevent them.

What changes

Cleaner first-pass claims

Scrubbing against payer rules before submission, so fewer claims come back at all.

AR that moves

Every ageing bucket worked on a schedule, with documented notes and visible escalation.

Denials that stop repeating

Root causes traced back to registration, documentation or coding and corrected there.

Predictable operating cost

Per-claim, per-seat or percentage-of-collections models, agreed up front.
