How these engagements work

These are process services rather than projects. You are buying trained capacity, documented procedures and a quality result, not a piece of software.

Each engagement is set up the same way. We learn your procedure and your exception handling, write it down as a work instruction, train a pod against it, run a paid pilot on live volume, and agree quality and turnaround targets before scaling.

Every file passes production and review. Quality is sampled beyond that, reported weekly, and reviewed with you monthly against the targets in the contract.

What is standard across all three

Named supervision
A team lead and a quality lead on every pod.

Two-level checking
Production plus review, with audit sampling above it.

Weekly reporting
Volume, turnaround, accuracy and exceptions.

Controlled access
Least-privilege systems access and a clean-desk operating model.

How quality and security are handled

Written work instructions

Your procedure documented, version controlled and used as the single reference for training and audit.

Calibration and golden files

New staff qualify against a known-answer set before touching live work, and are re-calibrated as rules change.

Quality measured, not asserted

Sampling rates and accuracy definitions agreed in the contract, with results published weekly.

Turnaround commitments

Standard and rush turnaround times per work type, tracked per file and reported against target.

Access control

Least-privilege access to your systems, individually named accounts, logged sessions and prompt revocation on staff changes.

Continuity

Cross-trained staff and documented procedures, so leave and attrition do not show up in your turnaround.
