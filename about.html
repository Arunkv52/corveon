Our story

Why Corveon exists

Corveon was founded by people who had spent years on the delivery side of enterprise programmes: cloud migrations, ERP rollouts, reporting platforms, offshore operations. The pattern repeated. Strategy was competent. Execution was where value leaked away.

The causes were consistent. Architecture decided without the operators in the room. Business systems configured to a textbook process nobody followed. Dashboards built on data nobody had cleaned. Support contracts that covered availability but not outcomes.

We set Corveon up to work the other way round. Start from the outcome, keep the same team from design through to running it, and be accountable for the parts most partners hand back: cutover, adoption, cost and the second and third year of operations.

How we work

Principles we hold to

⚙️

Design for the operator

Whoever runs the system on an ordinary Tuesday is in the design review. Operability is a requirement, not a phase.

🔁

Small increments, visible progress

Two-week cycles with something working at the end of each. Programmes fail quietly when nothing is demonstrable for a quarter.

📘

Documented, not tribal

Runbooks, architecture notes and process maps are part of delivery. You should be able to change partners without losing the plot.

💰

Honest about cost

Cloud spend, licence counts and support effort are estimated up front and reported against. Surprises are a failure of planning.

🔒

Security by default

Least privilege, encryption, logging and access reviews are built in from the first environment, not retrofitted before an audit.

🎯

Fewer, deeper accounts

We would rather run four platforms well than twelve badly. Capacity is planned before we sign, not after.
