Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information Corveon collects through this website and in the course of business, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Who we are

Corveon [Private Limited] (“Corveon”, “we”, “us”) operates corveon.in. For questions about this policy or about how your information is handled, write to hello@corveon.in.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need in order to respond to you and to run our business.

  • Information you give us: name, work email, phone number, company, job title and the details of your enquiry, submitted through our contact form or by email.
  • Recruitment information: the CV and application details you send to our careers address.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type and version, device type, pages visited, referring page and time of visit, collected through server logs and, where you consent, analytics cookies.
  • Client engagement information: contact details and correspondence relating to services we provide under contract.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide the information or proposal you asked for.
  • To deliver, support and administer services under a contract with you or your organisation.
  • To assess job applications.
  • To keep the website secure, diagnose faults and understand which pages are useful.
  • To send service or contractual notices, and marketing communications only where you have asked for them or where permitted by law, with an unsubscribe option in every message.
  • To meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

Legal bases

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on: your consent (for non-essential cookies and marketing); the performance of a contract (to deliver services); our legitimate interests (to respond to business enquiries, secure our systems and improve the site); and compliance with legal obligations.

Client data processed on your behalf

When delivering services we may process personal data that belongs to a client, for example within a CRM, ERP, billing or annotation workflow. In those cases the client is the controller and Corveon acts as a processor under a written agreement. We process such data only on the client’s instructions, apply the security controls set out in that agreement, and do not use it for our own purposes.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with: service providers who host our website, send our email or provide analytics; professional advisers such as auditors and lawyers; and authorities where the law requires it. Providers act under contract and only on our instructions.

International transfers

Corveon operates from India and works with clients and providers in other countries, including the United States and the European Union. Where information is transferred across borders, we use appropriate safeguards such as contractual protections with the receiving party.

Retention

Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months from last contact. Recruitment records are kept for up to 12 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Client engagement records are kept for the term of the contract and for the period afterwards required by law or by professional obligation. Server logs are kept for a short operational period.

Security

We apply access controls on a least-privilege basis, encryption in transit, individually named accounts, logging, staff confidentiality obligations and privacy training. No system is completely secure, but we work to a documented standard and review it.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, or provide it in a portable form. You may also withdraw consent where we relied on it. Write to hello@corveon.in and we will respond within the period the law allows. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the relevant data protection authority.

Cookies

This site uses a small number of cookies. Essential cookies are required for the site to function and to remember your cookie choice. Analytics cookies are used only with your consent. See the cookie policy for the detail and to change your preference at any time.

Children

This website is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.
