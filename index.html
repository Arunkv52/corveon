The Corveon model

The same five stages apply whether we are migrating a datacentre or standing up a billing pod. What changes is the depth of each stage.

01

Strategy

Understand the business outcome, the constraints and the sequence. Agree what success looks like in numbers before anything is built.

02

Build

Design and engineer the platform, system or process. Small increments, working software, no surprises at the end of a quarter.

03

Deploy

Migrate data, train people, cut over with a tested rollback. Go-live is an event we rehearse, not one we hope for.

04

Run

Operate to agreed service levels with named owners, monitoring and a monthly service review.

05

Optimise

Tune cost, performance and process once real usage data exists. Every quarter should leave the system better than the last.
