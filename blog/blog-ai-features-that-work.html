AI

AI features that customers actually use

PSPriya Suresh · Jul 29, 2026 · 7 min read
🤖
On this page
    Share
    LinkedInX

    Adding AI is easy. Adding AI that people trust and keep using is harder, and it starts with choosing the right problem.

    Choose a narrow, repeated job

    The best early features handle a specific task that people repeat often, such as answering policy questions, summarising tickets or drafting replies. Narrow jobs are easier to test and improve.

    Ground answers in your own data

    Connect the model to your documents and systems, and show sources with every answer. People forgive a missing answer far more easily than a confident wrong one.

    • Show where each answer came from
    • Let the assistant say when it does not know
    • Keep a human in the loop for risky actions

    Measure quality from day one

    Build a small set of real questions with known good answers and run it after every change. Track usage, cost and feedback so you know whether the feature earns its place.

    Key takeaway

    Start with a pilot for one team. Real usage will teach you more than any planning document.

    In short

    Useful AI is quiet: it saves a few minutes, many times a day.
